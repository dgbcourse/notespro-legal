Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information NotesPro collects, why, and how you can control it. NotesPro is a free app for browsing and downloading study notes.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, username, and email address when you sign up, handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage data — which notes you view, bookmark, download, and rate, plus your coin balance and completed daily tasks.
  • Referral data — your referral code and, if you used one, which member referred you.
  • Device token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, used only to deliver notifications about new notes.
  • Proof screenshots — images you choose to submit when claiming a bonus task, stored with a third-party image host.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your downloads and bookmarks, and track coins you've earned or spent.
  • To review and approve bonus-task submissions.
  • To send you notifications about new notes, if enabled.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob, which may use its own identifiers to personalise ads — see Google's own privacy policy for how AdMob handles data.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use these services to run the app, and each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and notifications.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads and rewards coins for watching them.
  • Cloudinary — stores screenshots you submit for bonus tasks.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile details any time from the app.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete Account — this removes your profile, download history, coins, and bookmarks permanently.
  • Turn off notifications from your device's app settings.

Children's privacy

NotesPro is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Common questions

A note won't open or download

Check your internet connection and try again from inside the app. If it still fails, report it via email with the note's name.

My coins didn't update after watching an ad

Coin credit can take a few seconds to confirm. If it's been more than a minute, reopen the Tasks tab — if it's still missing, email us with your username and the time it happened.

I want to delete my account

Go to Profile → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your data.
